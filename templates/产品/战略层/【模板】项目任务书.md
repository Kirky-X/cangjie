## 11. Risk and Exit Strategy

### 11.1 Risk Register

| Risk ID | Risk Description | Likelihood | Impact | Risk Level | Trigger Condition | Response Measure | Exit Condition |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **R-001** | Technical solution infeasible | Medium | High | 🔴 High | Technical POC failed | Introduce external experts / Adjust architecture / Switch tech stack | Delay > 2 months → pause |
| **R-002** | Key personnel turnover | Medium | High | 🔴 High | Critical position vacant > 2 weeks | Activate backup / External recruitment / Knowledge documentation | Unable to fill → scope reduction |
| **R-003** | Major requirement changes | High | Medium | 🟡 Medium | Change > 30% workload | Change control process / CCB review / Baseline freeze | Change > 50% → re-initiate project |
| **R-004** | Market acceptance below expectations | Medium | High | 🔴 High | MVP retention rate << 20% | Rapid Pivot / Focus on core scenarios / Adjust pricing | 2 consecutive months below target → terminate |
| **R-005** | Competitor fast follow | High | Medium | 🟡 Medium | Competitor launches similar features | Accelerate iteration / Build data barriers / Patent protection | Market share << 5% → adjust targets |
