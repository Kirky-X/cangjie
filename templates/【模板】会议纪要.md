## Usage Guidelines (Do not include in final minutes file)

### File Management

- **Naming Convention**: `【Minutes】{Topic}-{YYYY-MM-DD}-v{version}.md`
- **Storage Path**: `lark/minutes/{YYYY-MM}/`
- **Version Status**:
  - `v1.0-draft`: Initial draft sent within 1 hour after meeting
  - `v1.1-approved`: Final version after facilitator/key decision-maker review
  - `v1.2-final`: Archived final version after all action items completed

### Timeliness Requirements

| Phase | Deadline | Owner |
| ---------- | ----------------------- | ------------- |
| Draft Complete | Within **1 hour** after meeting | Note Taker |
| Attendee Confirmation | Within **4 hours** after draft sent | All attendees |
| Final Published | Within **24 hours** after meeting | Facilitator/Note Taker |
| Action Item Review | First **10 minutes** of next meeting | Facilitator |

### Writing Principles (FAR Principle)

1. **F - Focused (Result-oriented)**: Record decisions and actions, not conversation processes. Don't write "Zhang San thinks... Li Si rebutted..." Only write "the conclusion is..."
2. **A - Actionable**: Every action item must have four elements: "what to do, who does it, when to finish, how to verify completion."
3. **R - Referencable**: Meeting ID, action item ID, risk ID are globally unique; can be directly located in cross-meeting references.
4. **Closed-loop tracking**: Next meeting must review previous action item completion rate; target ≥ 80%. Incomplete items need reason explanation and new deadline.

### Simplification Guide (By Meeting Type)

| Meeting Type | Required Sections | Optional Sections | Can Delete/Simplify |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| **Project Kick-off** | Summary, Meeting Info, Agenda & Discussion (with decisions+actions), Risks, Next Meeting | Parking Lot | — |
| **Regular Standup** | Summary, Meeting Info, Agenda & Discussion (actions+risks only), Next Meeting | Parking Lot, Attachments | Detailed decision context (simplify to 1 sentence) |
| **Competitive/Research Analysis** | Summary, Meeting Info, Agenda & Discussion (info points+actions), Attachments | Parking Lot | Risks, detailed decisions |
| **Product Planning** | Summary, Meeting Info, Agenda & Discussion (decisions+actions+disagreements), Risks, Parking Lot | Attachments | — |
| **Brainstorming** | Meeting Info, Agenda & Discussion (record idea topics only, no decisions), Parking Lot, Next Meeting | — | Decisions, Risks, Attachments (unless decisions made on the spot) |
| **Decision Review** | Summary, Meeting Info, Agenda & Discussion (decisions+disagreements+actions), Risks | Parking Lot, Next Meeting | — |

### Quality Checklist (Pre-publication Self-check)

- [ ] Meeting summary allows non-attendees to understand core conclusions within 30 seconds
- [ ] Every decision has at least one numbered action item below it
- [ ] Every action item has clear "success criteria/acceptance metrics"
- [ ] All action item IDs are sequential, no gaps
- [ ] No vague time expressions like "ASAP" / "when appropriate"; all replaced with specific dates
- [ ] All subjective evaluations by note taker removed (e.g., "discussion was heated" / "everyone agreed")
- [ ] No important topics missed in Parking Lot
- [ ] File naming follows convention, version number correct
